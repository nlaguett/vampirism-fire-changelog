# Vampirism Fire 8.3.2

---

## Main

- Fixed two causes of desync.
- Fixed an issue that caused [Watch-Tower] to award **1500 XP** instead of **50 XP**.
- The Architect's [Advanced-Research-Center] now correctly awards XP.
- The Orc's [Topaz-Wall] now correctly awards XP.
- Fixed an issue where the **Archangel** wouldn't spawn from the **Blademaster Slayer**.
- Fixed an issue that might causes trees not regrowing at the min 4 mark.
- Switched default chat back to ally chat.
- [Vampiric-Boots] duration increased on hero unit from **30 -> 60 seconds**. Cooldown Decreased from **15 -> 10 seconds**.
