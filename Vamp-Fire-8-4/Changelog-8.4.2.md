# Vampirism Fire 8.4.2

---

## Vampires

- The vampire selection UI was improved: the title, vampire icons, and **ACCEPT** button no longer overlap the border.

    Before:

- [Sword-Of-Dracula]
    - Base damage increased from **1,750 to 2,150**.

- [Nosferatu's-Mantle]
    - Base damage increased from **0 to 3,000**, for a total of **4,250 damage**.

- [Punisher] now grants **+135 Strength** and **+115 Agility and Intelligence** per wall hit; previously, it granted only +135 Strength.

- [Nightmare's-Teller]
    - Can now also be built with **Stanimir's Nocturne** instead of **Nocturne's Edge**, plus one additional **Stanimir Remains** (or **Stanimir's Combined Remains**).
    - Previously, holding **Nocturne's Edge** when buying the first **Stanimir Remains** immediately transformed it into **Stanimir's Nocturne**, preventing the Teller from being built.
    - The Teller's stats are unchanged.
