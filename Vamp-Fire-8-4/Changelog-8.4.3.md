# Vampirism Fire 8.4.3

---

## Main

- On the race selection screen, players who are still choosing are now shown in their **player colors**.
- Training mode no longer adds a second **Gauntlets of Hellfire** and **Punisher** to the Demonic Gate.


## Humans

- Engineers (Human and Orc) can **fast-build** again: turning on Auto-Repair no longer roots a worker in place, so it walks to the construction site and helps.


## Vampires

- New shop: the **Vault of Remains**, a Skeletal Mage next to the Recipe Shop. It sells [Demonic-Remains], [Sword-Of-Dracula] and [Stanimir-Remains], including their minute 22 / 23 prices. The Demonic Gate keeps the four 2k items, the two Spheres of Doom and the Punisher.

- Demonic Gate fixes:
    - Fixed items **disappearing** from the Demonic Gate (Gauntlets of Hellfire, Gauntlets of the Underworld, Demonic Remains, Sword of Dracula, Stanimir Remains).
    - Fixed [Nosferatu's-Mantle] and [Nocturnes-Edge] appearing **twice** after another 2k item was bought.
    - Bought items now **stay in the shop**, greyed out with a countdown, and can be bought again after **3 minutes**.
    - [Gauntlets-Of-Hellfire] now opens at minute **35 -> 34**, together with [Nosferatu's-Mantle] and [Nocturnes-Edge]. Buying any of the four 2k items still puts the other three on cooldown, so a vampire can't buy two at once.
    - Gauntlets bought before they unlock are always the **real** gauntlets.
    - [Nosferatu's-Mantle] bought without a Sword of Dracula is refunded and can be **bought again right away** (it used to go on cooldown).

- New Demonic Gate layout:
    - Top row: **Gauntlets of the Underworld**, **Nosferatu's Mantle**, **Gauntlets of Hellfire**, **Nocturne's Edge**.
    - Second row: **Sphere of Doom**, **Silent Sphere of Doom**, **Punisher**.
    - Vault of Remains: **Demonic Remains**, **Sword of Dracula**, **Stanimir Remains**.

- Fixed stats **staying on the vampire** after dropping or selling an item bought at the Demonic Gate or the Vault.
- Fixed **Recipe - Nightmare's Teller** and [Gauntlets-Of-The-Damnation] appearing twice in the Recipe Shop after a purchase or a refund.
- Fixed the Black Market's **Large Claws of Attack +80** reset at minute 9: it is back in stock at 9:00, restocks one every 60 seconds, and no longer removes a different Black Market item.
- Vampire lumber income now starts at minute **10** in every mode (was 12, or 10 in Pro mode).

- Tooltip fixes:
    - [Sword-Of-Dracula] shows its real damage: **+2,150**.
    - [Nosferatu's-Mantle] shows its real damage: **+4,250**.
    - Removed outdated gauntlet notes and fixed several typos.
