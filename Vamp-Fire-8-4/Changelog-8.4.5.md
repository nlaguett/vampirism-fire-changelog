# Vampirism Fire 8.4.5

---

## Main

- The Ctrl+Q board now shows **Coins Picked** to **observers**.
- Fixed the Ctrl+Q **Chain Detos** column always showing 0.
- **Wins and ELO are now per player**: a builder who **died, left or became a minion** no longer gets a **win** when the builders win, and now **loses** ELO instead of gaining it. The longer they survived, the less they lose.
- **Minions** no longer get a win when the vampires win.
- Fixed ELO changes depending on the order of player slots: every player is now rated from the team ratings at the start of the game.


## Humans

- Flame tower damage increased (the 8.4.4 tooltips already showed these numbers):
    - **Tower of Flames**: 300 -> **325**
    - **Super Tower of Flames**: 800 -> **850**
    - **Ultimate Tower of Flames**: 3,200 -> **3,600**

- Building a **Vault** before minute 4 now regrows the trees around that base **right away**, instead of waiting for minute 4.
- **Barricade** now lasts **120 -> 90** seconds (Architect stays at 30 seconds).
- Human and Architect **Worker Motivation** now unlocks at **2:00 -> 3:30**, and the research is now **instant** (still 2,000 lumber).


## Orc

- Orc Slayer upgrade [Brute-Strength] now matches the Human and Architect Slayer Adept Training:
    - Same stats per level (HP, damage, armor, mana).
    - Research time is now **10/30/50** seconds.
    - Lumber cost reduced from 1,200 -> 1,000.
- Orc Slayer now gets the **Tracker** ability at levels 5, 12 and 19, like the other Slayers.


## Vampires

- [Veil-Of-Darkness] cooldown reduced from **120/60/30 -> 60/30/10** seconds.
- **Shattering** can no longer destroy **invulnerable** buildings (such as buildings under the Slayer's Building Protection).
